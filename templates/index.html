PROJECT INFORMATION

About SpiralSense

Project overview, model approach, and responsible-use information.

Academic research
01

What is SpiralSense?

SpiralSense is an academic research prototype that explores AI-assisted screening of Parkinson’s disease from spiral hand-drawing images. It provides a model prediction and a visual explanation to help researchers inspect model behavior.

02

Model approach

The project uses an EfficientNetV2-S image-classification model. The application reports the existing model output, calibrated probability, and uncertainty-related indicators from its current inference pipeline.

03

Explainability

Grad-CAM produces a heatmap that highlights image regions associated with the model’s prediction. It can help visualize model behavior, but it does not establish causation or clinical validity.

04

Responsible use and limitations

SpiralSense is a research prototype, not a medical device. Predictions may be wrong or uncertain and must not be used alone to diagnose, rule out, or make treatment decisions about Parkinson’s disease.
